# Marketing playbook — the 90-day plan

`MARKETING.md` in the root is the strategy. This is the schedule: what to do each week, in
order, with the exact posting cadence.

**Assumption:** 4–6 hours a week, alongside a job. Adjust the calendar, not the order.

---

## The 90 days at a glance

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Done when |
|-------|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Foundation** | 1–2 | Set up, start posting while still writing | 10 posts up, list live |
| **Rhythm** | 3–6 | Consistency, learning what lands | 30 posts, a format that works |
| **Launch** | 7–8 | Page live, first sales | Checkout tested, launch done |
| **Compound** | 9–12 | Volume, conversations, iteration | 60+ posts, evaluation point |

**Do not evaluate before week 12.** Posts 1–10 get nothing. That is universal, and it is
where most people stop.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (weeks 1–2)

You are still writing the course. Start posting anyway — distribution has a long lag, and
starting on launch day means launching to nobody.

### Week 1

**Monday — setup, 60 min**
- [ ] Pick **one** platform (`vault/04-Market/02-Choosing-Your-Platform.md`)
- [ ] Bio: who you help, with what. Not "entrepreneur | dreamer"
- [ ] Switch to a creator/business account for analytics
- [ ] One link, pointing at email capture

**Tuesday — email list, 45 min**
- [ ] Sign up: MailerLite or Buttondown
- [ ] Lead magnet: a **one-page** checklist from your course material
- [ ] Form live, link in bio
- [ ] Write the welcome email

**Wednesday — ideas, 45 min**
- [ ] 40 post ideas from your lesson titles, action items and `Their Words.md`
- [ ] 30 hooks in `Hooks.md` (`vault/04-Market/03-Hooks.md`)

**Thursday — batch, 90 min**
- [ ] Write 5 scripts
- [ ] Film all 5
- [ ] Edit, add captions

**Friday — first post**
- [ ] Post one. Reply to every comment.
- [ ] Expect nothing. Getting nothing is the correct week-1 outcome.

### Week 2

- **Post 5 times.** Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri.
- **Reply to every comment**, 20 min daily.
- **Sunday:** review numbers, plan week 3, write 5 more scripts.
- **Monday:** batch-film 5.

**End of phase 1:** 10 posts, email capture live, a working rhythm.

## Phase 2 — Rhythm (weeks 3–6)

Finish the course. Keep posting. Learn what lands.

### The weekly loop — repeat for four weeks

| Day | Time | Do |
|-----|------|-----|
| Sun | 60 min | Review numbers → fill calendar → write 5 scripts |
| Mon | 90 min | Film 5 → edit 5 → schedule |
| Daily | 20 min | Post, reply to every comment and DM |
| — | rest | Write the course |

### The content mix

Each week of five posts:

| Posts | Type |
|-------|------|
| 2–3 | Teach one thing |
| 1 | Show the problem (before/after) |
| 1 | Personal — a mistake you made |
| occasional | Answer a question from comments |

**Product mentions: one in twenty posts.** Not one in five.

### What to watch, weekly

| Number | Target | If below |
|--------|--------|----------|
| 3-second retention | 60%+ | **Fix hooks. Nothing else matters** |
| Shares | any | Study your top 3 — that is your format |
| Comments | any | Ask questions at the end of posts |
| Email signups | 1–2/week | Lead magnet too vague, or close too weak |

**One change per week.** Changing five things means learning nothing.

### Week 4 checkpoint

~20 posts. Ask:

- Is retention above 60%? If not, every hour goes into hooks.
- Which format got the most shares? Make more of that.
- Have you had a real conversation? If not, start ending posts with questions.

### Week 6 — get ahead

Build a **one-week buffer** before launch. Launch week eats your time, and a gap in posting
during launch is the worst possible timing.

## Phase 3 — Launch (weeks 7–8)

### Week 7 — build the machine

Course finished, three readers have given feedback
(`vault/02-Build/09-Packaging-And-Shipping.md`).

- **Mon–Tue:** sales page. Use `vault/Templates/07-Sales-Page-Skeleton.md`
- **Wed:** Stripe setup end to end (`SETUP.md`)
- **Thu:** email service + SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Score 9+ at mail-tester.com
- **Fri:** **buy your own course with a real card.** Refund yourself. Fix what you find.
- **Sat:** `SECURITY.md` checklist. Go live with live keys.

Keep posting throughout. Five a week, from your buffer.

### Week 8 — launch

**Launch is not one moment. It is a week.**

| Day | Do |
|-----|-----|
| Mon | Post: "I've been building something." Show the process, not the product |
| Tue | Post: the problem it solves. Still no pitch |
| Wed | **Launch post.** What it is, who it is for, who it is *not* for. Link |
| Wed | Email your list. Same message, longer |
| Thu | Post: answer the first objection you received |
| Fri | Post: behind the scenes — what is actually inside |
| Sat/Sun | Reply to everything. Every comment, every DM |

### If you want a launch price, make it real

```
LAUNCH_PRICE_ENDS_AT=2026-11-15T23:59:59Z
PRICE_METHOD_AFTER_LAUNCH=7900
```

The server enforces it. **Then actually let it happen.** Extending a deadline you announced
teaches everyone watching that your deadlines are fiction, and costs more than the
extension gains.

### Expect a quiet launch

Most first launches produce 0–5 sales. That is not a verdict on the product — it is a
reflection of how many people have seen you.

**The work now is distribution, not another product.**

## Phase 4 — Compound (weeks 9–12)

Same weekly loop. Four more weeks. This is where it either starts working or you learn
something specific.

### Add in week 9

- **Reddit.** Find three threads asking your question. Answer properly, at length, **no
  link.** Repeat weekly. Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules.
- **Other people's comments.** Five larger accounts in your niche, one genuinely useful
  reply each per week. Their audience sees it. Enormously underrated.

### Add in week 11

- **Ask for feedback.** The day-45 email goes to your earliest customers. Real testimonials
  start here (`vault/05-Bonus/04-Collecting-Testimonials.md`).
- **Check attribution.** The dashboard's by-source table shows which platform produces
  *buyers*, not just views. Often different.

### Week 12 — the evaluation point

60+ posts. Now you may draw conclusions.

Work the diagnostic ladder in `vault/04-Market/09-When-Nothing-Works.md`:

| Views | Conversion | Diagnosis |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Low | — | Hooks. Fix only that |
| High | ~0, no clicks | Wrong audience — make content closer to the problem |
| High | Low, clicks happening | Sales page |
| Low | High | **It works. Needs volume.** Post more |
| High | Good | Working. Do more of it |

## Channel playbooks

### Instagram Reels

- 5–7/week. Captions always. 3–5 hashtags, no more.
- **Reply to every comment** — Instagram's DM culture is the strongest sales channel of any
  short-form platform.
- Story after each Reel to push it to existing followers.
- Do not automate DMs before post 60. The manual conversations *are* the strategy.

### TikTok

- 5–10/week — highest volume tolerance of any platform.
- Trends matter more than elsewhere, but do not force one that does not fit.
- Younger and more price-sensitive. Expect lower conversion per view.
- Reply with video to good comments — TikTok distributes those separately.

### YouTube Shorts → long-form

- Shorts to build, long-form to convert.
- **Long-form has the best longevity of anything here** — a ranking video brings viewers
  for years.
- Title and thumbnail do 80% of the work on long-form.
- Description with your link and timestamps.

### LinkedIn (B2B topics)

- 3–5 text posts/week. Video underperforms text here.
- First two lines are the hook — everything else is behind "see more".
- Reply to every comment within an hour of posting; it drives distribution hard.

### Pinterest (visual / how-to topics)

- Consistently underrated. It is a search engine people forget is a search engine.
- Pins surface for **months**, not days.
- 5–10 pins/week, keyword-rich descriptions.
- Far lower competition than video platforms.

### Reddit

- **Participate for weeks before mentioning anything you sell.**
- Answer properly and at length. Profile checks follow good answers.
- Read each subreddit's rules on self-promotion — they vary and they are enforced.
- One of the best places to find early customers, and the easiest to get removed from.

## What not to do

- **Do not buy followers or engagement.** It corrupts your only feedback signal.
- **Do not run ads before you know your page converts.** Paid traffic amplifies; it does
  not fix. You would be paying to learn what free traffic teaches you.
- **Do not post to four platforms at once.** Mediocre on all, learning from none.
- **Do not optimise posting times or hashtags.** Content decides reach.
- **Do not build a second product** because the first one is quiet. Usually you need more
  people to see the first one, not another one.
- **Do not make income claims.** See `vault/01-Mindset/06-Ethics-And-Claims.md`. The safe
  default is none.

## Support response templates

Your support quality is marketing. A fast, gracious reply produces referrals; a slow one
produces chargebacks.

Full set: `vault/Templates/03-Support-Replies.md`. The standard:

- **Reply within 24 hours.** That is the whole commitment.
- **Unblock first, debug second.**
- **Never argue about a refund.**
- **Every repeated question is a documentation bug** — fix the FAQ so the next person never
  asks.

`Weekly-Numbers.md`, one row a week:

| Week | Posts | Best 3s retention | Shares | Clicks | Signups | Sales | One change |
|------|-------|-------------------|--------|--------|---------|-------|------------|

Patterns only appear across weeks. Memory is unreliable about which post did what.

**Sample sizes before concluding anything:**
- 10 posts of a format
- 200 visitors before judging a page
- 60 posts before judging the whole thing
