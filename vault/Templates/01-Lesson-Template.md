# Lesson template

Copy everything between the lines into each new lesson file.

Full explanation of why each part exists: [[02-Build/04-Anatomy-Of-A-Lesson]].

---

```markdown
# [N.N] — [Specific title, stated as a claim or instruction]

**[X] minutes · Module [N] · [[00-Module-Overview|Back to module]]**

---

## [Hook heading — the failure, the contradiction, or the question]

[2–4 sentences. Make the cost of not knowing this concrete.

 Do NOT open with a definition.
 Do NOT open with "In this lesson we will...".
 Do open by naming something true about their situation.]

---

## [Specific section heading, not "Overview"]

[Teaching. Short paragraphs, 2–4 sentences.
 Bold the one load-bearing sentence per section.
 Use a table any time you're comparing two things.]

## [Another specific heading]

[At least one concrete example per lesson — ideally your own mistake.
 See [[02-Build/07-Examples-And-Cases]].]

## [Show the wrong version]

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| [what most people do] | [what works] |

## When this doesn't apply

[Counter-example. Builds enormous trust — it shows you know the boundary
 of your own advice.]

---

## Action Items

1. **[Verb] [specific thing].** [Names an output — a file, a photo, a list.]
2. **[Verb] [specific thing].** [Completable in under 20 minutes.]
3. **[Verb] [specific thing].** [Uses what this lesson just taught.]

[3–5 items. Smallest first — someone who does item one usually does item two.]

---

**Next:** [[NN-Next-Lesson|N.N — Title]]

**Related:** [[other-lesson|N.N]] · [[another|N.N]]
```

---

## The checks before you call a lesson done

- [ ] Reading time at the top
- [ ] Hook makes the cost concrete — and isn't a definition
- [ ] Every heading is specific, not "Overview" or "Introduction"
- [ ] At least one concrete example
- [ ] Read only the bold sentences — do they carry the argument?
- [ ] 3–5 action items, each naming an output
- [ ] Links to next, and to 2–3 related lessons
- [ ] 600–1,200 words
- [ ] Read aloud — no sentence you stumble over
