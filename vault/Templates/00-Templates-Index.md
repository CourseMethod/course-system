**Copy each one into your own vault before editing**, so you always have the clean version
to come back to.

## A note on using these

These are structures, not scripts. **Rewrite them in your own voice.**

A template used verbatim reads as a template, and your reader has seen the same one from
four other people. What makes them valuable is that the thinking about *order and purpose*
is already done — which sentence does what job. Keep the skeleton, replace the words.
