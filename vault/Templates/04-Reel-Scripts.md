# Reel script templates

Five formats that work, as fill-in skeletons. Full explanation:
[[04-Market/04-Reel-Scripts]].

**Target: 25–45 seconds, roughly 2.5 words per second.** A 35-second video is about 85
words. That is short, and the shortness is the discipline.

---

## The shape

```
0–3s    HOOK     Create the gap
3–8s    SETUP    Why it matters / what's wrong
8–30s   PAYOFF   The actual answer
30–40s  CLOSE    One next step
```

**Most of the time is payoff.** The common failure is twenty seconds of setup and five of
answer.

---

## Format 1 — The fix

Most reliable. Use it for most posts.

```
HOOK    "[THEIR PROBLEM, STATED AS FACT]. It's not [WHAT THEY BLAME]."

SETUP   "It's [ACTUAL CAUSE]. [ONE SENTENCE ON WHY]."

PAYOFF  "[THE FIX, IN STEPS.]
         [SHOW IT HAPPENING.]
         [SHOW THE RESULT.]"

CLOSE   "[ONE NEXT STEP]"
```

---

## Format 2 — The mistake list

Excellent comment generator — people self-identify.

```
HOOK    "[N] things making your [THING] look [BAD ADJECTIVE].
         First one:"

PAYOFF  "1. [MISTAKE] — [ONE-LINE FIX]
         2. [MISTAKE] — [ONE-LINE FIX]
         3. [MISTAKE] — [ONE-LINE FIX]
         4. [MISTAKE] — [ONE-LINE FIX]"
         [~5 seconds each, each one shown]

CLOSE   "Which one are you doing?"
```

---

## Format 3 — Before and after

Highest retention if your topic is visual. **The first frame is the hook.**

```
HOOK    [FIRST FRAME SHOWS THE CONTRAST]
        "Same [THING]. [SHORT TIME] apart."

SETUP   "The only difference is [ONE VARIABLE]."

PAYOFF  [SHOW THE CHANGE BEING MADE, IN REAL TIME]

CLOSE   "Takes [TIME]. Costs nothing."
```

---

## Format 4 — The confession

Builds trust fast, because it costs you something to say.

```
HOOK    "I spent [AMOUNT/TIME] on [THING]. [WHAT HAPPENED TO IT]."

SETUP   "I thought [WRONG BELIEF]. Actually [TRUTH]."

PAYOFF  "[WHAT ACTUALLY SOLVED IT]"

CLOSE   "Save the [AMOUNT/TIME]."
```

---

## Format 5 — The answered question

Invites DMs, which is where early sales come from.

```
HOOK    "Someone asked why [THEIR EXACT QUESTION, IN THEIR WORDS]."

PAYOFF  "[THE ANSWER]"

CLOSE   "Send me yours if you want a look."
```

Take the question verbatim from `Their Words.md`
([[01-Mindset/04-Finding-Who-Its-For]]).

## Hook bank — fill these in

Write 30 in one sitting. Nine out of ten will be weak; that is the expected ratio.

| Pattern | Template |
|---------|----------|
| Specific mistake | "Your [THING] [PROBLEM]. It's not [WHAT THEY BLAME]." |
| Contradiction | "Stop [OBVIOUS ACTION]. It won't fix this." |
| Cost | "This one [SMALL THING] cost me [BIG AMOUNT]." |
| Before/after | "This is the same [THING]. [TIME] apart." |
| Their question | "Why does your [THING] [PROBLEM]?" |
| Number | "[N] things make [THING] look [BAD]. Here's the first." |
| Confession | "I spent [AMOUNT] on [THING]. [WHAT HAPPENED]." |
| Direct address | "If you [SPECIFIC SITUATION], this is why [PROBLEM]." |

---

## Closes

One per post. Not three.

| Goal | Close |
|------|-------|
| Build the list | "Free [LEAD MAGNET] — link in bio." |
| Start conversations | "Comment '[WORD]' and I'll send it." |
| Get engagement | "Which one are you doing?" |
| Invite DMs | "Send me yours if you want a look." |
| Sell | "Full course in bio." |

**Early on, prefer conversation and list-building over selling.** Sell to people who have
seen you five times, not once.

---

## Pre-post checklist

- [ ] Hook works in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Hook works in all three modes: spoken, on-screen text, opening visual
- [ ] Captions on — most viewing is muted
- [ ] Under 45 seconds
- [ ] Payoff starts before second 10
- [ ] Filler stripped ("so basically", "one of the things that", "at the end of the day")
- [ ] One close, not three
- [ ] No competitor watermark
- [ ] UTM tag on the bio link ([[04-Market/08-Reading-Analytics]])
