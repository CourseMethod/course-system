# Sales page skeleton

Fourteen sections, in order. Each answers one of the seven questions a sceptical stranger
has. Full reasoning: [[03-Sell/03-The-Sales-Page]].

**A reader leaves at the first unanswered question.** That is the diagnostic — when the
page underperforms, find which question it fails to answer.

---

## The seven questions

1. What is this?
2. Is it for me?
3. What exactly do I get?
4. Why should I believe you?
5. What does it cost?
6. What if it's wrong for me?
7. What happens when I click?

---

## 1. Headline

> **[SOMETHING TRUE ABOUT THEIR SITUATION, STATED MORE PRECISELY THAN THEY EXPECT]**

Not what you sell. What is true about them.

- ✗ "The Complete [TOPIC] Course"
- ✓ "Your [THING] looks better in person than in your photos. That's fixable."

Write ten. Pick the most specific, not the most exciting.

## 2. Sub-headline

> [WHAT IT IS, PLAINLY, IN ONE SENTENCE.]

> e.g. "A 20-lesson course on [DOING X] with [MINIMAL RESOURCES]."

## 3. Primary CTA — above the fold

Some readers arrive already convinced. Do not make them scroll past your whole argument.

## 4. Trust line

> ✓ One payment, yours forever ✓ [N]-day refund, no questions ✓ No subscription

## 5. The problem

> [DESCRIBE THEIR SITUATION ACCURATELY ENOUGH THAT THEY FEEL UNDERSTOOD.]
>
> [NAME THE 3–4 SPECIFIC PLACES IT STOPS FOR THEM.]

Do not manufacture pain. Accuracy is the persuasion.

## 6. What this actually is

> [CONCRETE. FILE FORMAT, LESSON COUNT, HOW IT OPENS, WHAT IT DOESN'T INCLUDE.]

The strongest trust-builder available to a seller with no testimonials: describe the
product so precisely it is obvious you built it.

## 7. What's inside

> **[ALL LESSON TITLES. ALL OF THEM. NOT A TEASER.]**

Feels like giving too much away. It is not — titles are not the course, and a buyer who
can see exactly what they get converts far better. It also filters wrong-fit buyers, which
lowers your refund rate.

## 8. Who it's for / who it isn't

| ✓ It fits if | It doesn't fit if |
|--------------|-------------------|
| [CONDITION] | [HONEST DISQUALIFICATION] |
| [CONDITION] | [HONEST DISQUALIFICATION] |
| [CONDITION] | [HONEST DISQUALIFICATION] |

**The right column is the highest-trust section on the page.** Do not soften it.

## 9. Proof

**If you have real, consented testimonials** ([[05-Bonus/04-Collecting-Testimonials]]) —
show them, with real names, and only mark verified what you have evidenced.

**If you don't** — say so:

> I don't have testimonials yet. I could write some. Plenty of pages do. I'd rather tell
> you what I actually have instead.
>
> [THE PRODUCT IS INSPECTABLE — full contents above]
> [THE RISK SITS WITH ME — unconditional refund]
> [WHEN REAL RESULTS COME IN, THEY GO HERE — not before]

**Never invent testimonials.** Illegal, spotted, and the honest version converts better.

## 10. Pricing

Cards, then a comparison table, then one paragraph telling them which to pick and why.

## 11. Guarantee — its own section

> **[N] days. No conditions. No questions.**
>
> Email me and I'll refund you. I won't ask what went wrong, won't ask you to prove you
> tried, won't route you through retention emails. One reply, refunded within a business
> day.
>
> **You keep the files.**

Conditional "action-based" guarantees backfire — they signal you expect to be asked and
want it to be hard. See [[03-Sell/09-Refunds-And-Support]].

## 12. FAQ

Handle the **real** objections, not logistics. Answer the question underneath the
question:

| They ask | They mean | Your answer |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| "How long is it?" | "Will I finish it?" | |
| "Is this for beginners?" | "Will I be lost?" | |
| "What makes you qualified?" | "Are you a fraud?" | |
| "Why so cheap?" | "What's wrong with it?" | |
| "Can't I find this free?" | "Am I being ripped off?" | |
| "[YOUR BIGGEST OBJECTION]" | | |

Answer honestly, including when the honest answer is unflattering. Address the *strongest*
version of the objection.

## 13. Final CTA

> ## [ONE LINE RESTATING THE CORE IDEA]
> [ONE BUTTON]
> [N]-day refund, no questions. One payment, yours permanently.

## 14. Footer

Support contact · Refund request · Privacy · Terms · **Results disclaimer**

> **On results:** this page makes no income claim. What you make depends on your topic,
> your audience, your execution and your persistence. Many people who buy a course like
> this earn nothing from it, usually because they don't finish. Any testimonials shown are
> real, published with permission, and reflect that individual's experience rather than a
> typical result.

Required if you make any outcome claim anywhere. See [[01-Mindset/06-Ethics-And-Claims]].

---

## Final checks

- [ ] Every section answers a different question — no repetition
- [ ] Full lesson list shown
- [ ] Honest disqualification section present
- [ ] No claim a worse competitor could also write
- [ ] No invented testimonials, no fake countdown, no fictitious "was" price
- [ ] Read aloud — nothing sounds like an advert
- [ ] Works on a phone
- [ ] Checkout tested with a real card
