## The mix

| Type | Share | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| Teach one thing | 50% | The core |
| Show the problem | 20% | Before/after, what wrong looks like |
| Personal | 15% | Your mistakes. Builds trust |
| Answer a question | 10% | From comments, DMs, Reddit |
| Mention the product | 5% | **One in twenty. No more.** |

That last row is deliberately small. An account that mostly sells gets unfollowed and
stops being distributed.

---

## Where the ideas come from

You already wrote them:

1. **Every lesson title** → at least one post
2. **Every action item** → a "do this today" post
3. **Every example and mistake** → a confession post
4. **`Course Two.md`** → cut material is free content
5. **`Their Words.md`** → every question is a post
6. **Your support inbox** → every repeated question is a post

Half an hour with these produces 40 ideas.

## The weekly rhythm

**Sunday, 60 min**
- Review last week's numbers
- Fill next week's rows
- Write five scripts

**Monday, 90 min**
- Film all five
- Edit all five
- Schedule them

**Daily, 20 min**
- Reply to every comment and DM

That daily twenty minutes is where the sales come from
([[04-Market/06-Comments-And-DMs]]). It is not optional.

## Rules

- **Be one week ahead at all times.** A buffer means a bad week costs nothing visible.
- **Ten posts of a format before judging it.** One post is noise.
- **Sixty posts before judging the whole thing.** Most people stop at twelve.
- **Do not optimise posting times or hashtags.** Three to five relevant hashtags, a time
  you can hit consistently. The content decides the reach.
