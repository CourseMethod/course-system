## Read this first (2 minutes)

There are 40 lessons in here. **Do not read them all before starting.**

That instruction matters more than anything else on this page. The people who finish
and launch are not the ones who read fastest — they are the ones who read one lesson,
do the thing at the bottom of it, and only then open the next. People who binge the
whole vault over a weekend feel enormously productive and have built nothing by Monday.

Every lesson ends with **Action Items**. Those are the course. The words above them are
just context for doing them.

## The order

Follow it top to bottom. The modules are sequenced because each depends on a decision
made in the one before.

| # | Module | Lessons | What you have at the end |
|---|--------|---------|--------------------------|
| 01 | [[01-Mindset/00-Module-Overview\|Mindset]] | 8 | A topic you have stopped second-guessing |
| 02 | [[02-Build/00-Module-Overview\|Build]] | 9 | A finished course, written |
| 03 | [[03-Sell/00-Module-Overview\|Sell]] | 9 | A live page that takes money |
| 04 | [[04-Market/00-Module-Overview\|Market]] | 9 | People arriving at that page |
| 05 | [[05-Bonus/00-Module-Overview\|Beyond the first launch]] | 5 | A business rather than an event |

**Do not touch Module 3 or 4 until you have something to sell.** Building the sales
machine before the product is the single most common way this goes wrong — it feels like
progress, it is measurable and satisfying, and at the end you have a beautiful checkout
for a course that does not exist.

## Start here, right now

1. Read [[01-Mindset/01-Why-This-Works|1.1 — Why this works]] — 6 minutes.
2. Do [[01-Mindset/02-Picking-Your-Topic|1.2 — Picking your topic]] — 12 minutes, and it is
   the decision everything else hangs on.
3. Create a note called `My Course.md` in this vault and put your answer in it.

That third step is not optional busywork. Keep `My Course.md` open as you work through
the vault and dump every idea into it as you go. By the end of Module 2 that scratch file
has quietly become your outline, and you never face a blank page.

## A realistic timeline

At **4–6 hours a week**, with evenings free:

| Week | What you are doing | Done when |
|------|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Module 1, then start Module 2 | Topic chosen, outline drafted |
| 2–3 | Module 2 | Lessons written. Rough is correct at this stage |
| 4 | Module 3 | Page live, checkout working, one test purchase made |
| 5–8 | Module 4 | Posting consistently, talking to people who reply |

If you are slower than this, you are not behind. It is a pace, not a deadline. What
matters is the order, not the calendar.

What this timeline does **not** promise is income by week 8. Building takes weeks.
Distribution takes months. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling you a feeling.

## The five things that actually determine whether this works

Everything in the vault reduces to these.

1. **You pick a topic and stop reconsidering it.** Most stalls are here.
2. **You write the lessons.** The writing is the product. There is no way around it.
3. **You publish before it is good.** Version one is always worse than version two,
   and you cannot get to version two without shipping version one.
4. **You post for 30 days before judging the result.** The first ten posts get no
   traction. That is not failure, it is the beginning.
5. **You talk to the people who reply.** Every early sale comes from a conversation.
