# Module 3 — Sell

**9 lessons · about 100 minutes · a live page that takes money at the end**

---

## Before you start

**Do not begin this module until you have a finished draft.** If you have come here from
Module 1 because sales pages are more interesting than lesson four, go back.

Building the selling machine before the product exists is the most seductive way this
project fails. It feels productive, it is highly visible, and it ends with a beautiful
checkout for a course nobody wrote.

---

## What this module is for

At the end you have:

- A price you can defend
- A live sales page
- A working Stripe checkout you have tested with a real card
- Automatic delivery, so a purchase at 3am is handled without you
- The email sequence that follows a sale
- A refund policy and the means to honour it quickly

## The two lessons people regret skipping

**3.5 — Honest urgency.** Fake countdowns and invented scarcity are deceptive practices,
trivially detected, and a fraud signal to payment processors. This lesson shows how to get
the conversion benefit legitimately.

**3.9 — Refunds and support.** Handling refunds badly converts a mild disappointment into
a chargeback, and chargebacks threaten your payment account in a way refunds never do.

---

## The shortcut, if you want one

You do not have to build any of the machinery. **Gumroad, Payhip or Lemon Squeezy will
take payments and deliver a file within an afternoon**, in exchange for a percentage of
every sale.

For your first few sales that is genuinely the sensible choice, and this module says so
in [[06-Stripe-Setup]] rather than pretending otherwise. Build your own when you want to
stop paying a percentage forever and own the customer relationship.
