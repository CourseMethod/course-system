# Module 2 — Build

**9 lessons · about 100 minutes of reading · a finished course at the end**

---

## What this module is for

You finish this module with a written course. Not an outline, not a plan — actual
lessons, in actual files, that someone could read and learn from.

This is the module where the work happens. Modules 1, 3 and 4 are decisions and systems.
This one is writing, and there is no way around it: **the writing is the product**.

---

## Before you start

You should already have, from [[01-Mindset/00-Module-Overview|Module 1]]:

- A topic you have stopped reconsidering
- A specific person you are writing for
- A scope commitment with a real deadline
- `My Course.md`, `Their Words.md` and `Course Two.md` in your vault

If any of those is missing, go back. Writing without them is how people produce 8,000
words they later delete.

## The order to actually work in

Do not read all nine then start. Interleave:

1. Read 2.1 and 2.2, set up your vault. **One evening.**
2. Read 2.3, build your outline. **One evening.**
3. Read 2.4, write **one** lesson start to finish. **One evening.**
4. Read 2.5 and 2.6, then write the rest. **Two to three weeks.**
5. Read 2.7 and 2.8 while editing, and apply them to what you have.
6. Read 2.9 when the draft is done.

Step 3 matters. Writing one complete lesson before writing twenty tells you whether your
structure works, and fixing it after one is trivial.

---

## The two failure modes

**Reading instead of writing.** You will be tempted to read ahead into Module 3 because
sales pages are more fun than lesson four. Do not. Module 3 is locked until you have a
draft.

**Polishing instead of finishing.** First drafts are meant to be bad. You cannot edit
something that does not exist, and the editing pass in 2.7 and 2.8 is where quality
actually comes from.
