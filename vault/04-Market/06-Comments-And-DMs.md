# 4.6 — Comments, DMs and conversations

**10 minutes · Module 4 · [[00-Module-Overview|Back to module]]**

---

## Where your first sales actually come from

Not the viral post. Almost nobody's first sale comes from a big view count.

It comes from this sequence:

> Someone comments → you reply properly → they DM you → you have a real exchange →
> they buy

That is the shape of nearly every early sale in this business. Which means **replying is
not an optional extra, it is the main activity** — and the twenty minutes a day it takes
is the highest-return time you will spend.

A post with 400 views and six real conversations is worth more right now than one with
40,000 views and no replies.

---

## Reply to everything, for the first 90 days

Every comment. Including "nice", including emoji.

Two reasons:

**Engagement compounds.** Replies count as engagement and keep a post circulating. A post
with forty comments where half are your replies performs better than one with twenty.

**People notice.** Being replied to by a person with 200 followers feels different from
shouting at an account with a million. That difference is your entire advantage at this
stage, and you lose it the moment you get too big — so use it while you have it.

---

## What a good reply looks like

Add something. A reply that adds nothing gets you the engagement and none of the
relationship.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Thanks!" | "Thanks — the ceiling light thing surprised me too when I found it." |
| "Good question" | "Good question. Short answer: yes, but only if the window faces north." |
| "DM me" | "Depends on the product — what are you shooting? Happy to look." |
| "Check my bio" | "I go through this properly in one of my lessons, but the quick version is: [answer]" |

The pattern: **answer the question in the comment**, then optionally open a door.

Do not withhold the answer to force a DM. It is transparent and it irritates people. Give
the answer; the people who want more will ask.

---

## Turning a comment into a conversation

The move is a **genuine question back**.

> Them: "This is helpful, my photos always look yellow."
> You: "Yellow usually means a warm bulb somewhere. Are you shooting in the evening?"

You have asked something specific and answerable. Most people reply. Now it is a
conversation rather than a comment thread, and conversations are where trust forms.

Do not pitch here. You are three exchanges away from that and pitching early ends it.

---

## DMs

### When they DM you first

Ideal. They have opted in. Be useful, be brief, and be a person.

**Do not open with a pitch.** Answer their question. If it turns out your course is
genuinely the answer, say so plainly, once:

> "Honestly, the full version of this is in my course — it's £47 and it covers the whole
> range-consistency problem. But the quick answer to yours is: [answer]. That'll fix most
> of it on its own."

Answer given regardless. Offer made once. No follow-up pressure. If they want it they
will ask, and if they do not you have still helped someone who now thinks well of you.

### Cold DMs

Do not. Unsolicited sales DMs get you reported, muted and occasionally restricted, and the
conversion is terrible.

The exception: replying to someone's public question with genuine help, having never
mentioned your product. That is participating, not pitching.

---

## Automated DM tools

You will see "comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the link" funnels, usually via ManyChat or
similar.

**They work.** They are also a trade worth making consciously:

| Gain | Cost |
|------|------|
| Scales past what you can reply to by hand | Feels automated, because it is |
| Comment volume boosts the post | Loses the conversation that actually sells |
| Captures emails at scale | Some platforms restrict aggressive use |

**Do not automate before post 60.** Early on, the manual conversations *are* the strategy,
and automating them away removes the mechanism that produces your first customers.

If you do automate later, make the first automated message useful rather than a bare
link, and make it obvious a real reply is possible.

---

## Where else to have conversations

Your own comments are not the only place, and early on they are thin.

**Reddit.** Find threads asking your exact question and answer them properly, at length,
with no link. Do this for weeks. It builds genuine standing, and people check profiles.
Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules — they vary and they are enforced.

**Other people's comments.** Find larger accounts in your niche and leave genuinely useful
replies on their posts. Not "great post 🔥" — an actual contribution. Their audience sees
it. This is one of the most underrated growth activities available and it costs nothing.

**Facebook groups and Discords** where your audience gathers. Same rule: participate for
weeks before mentioning anything.

The unifying principle: **be useful in public, consistently, where your people already
are.** It works slowly and it works reliably.

---

## Handling criticism

You will get some. Three kinds:

**Genuine disagreement.** Engage properly. "Fair point — that's true for flat artwork.
For textured products it goes the other way because..." This is good content and it makes
you look like someone who knows the boundaries of their own advice.

**Bad faith.** "This is a scam." Reply once, calmly, factually, then stop. Onlookers are
the audience, not the commenter. One measured reply reads well; an argument does not.

**Abuse.** Delete and block. No engagement, no thought spent on it.

---

## Action Items

1. **Commit to replying to every comment** for 90 days. Put twenty minutes a day in your
   calendar.
2. **Practise the question-back.** On your next ten comments, end at least five with a
   specific question.
3. **Find five larger accounts** in your niche. Leave one genuinely useful comment on each
   this week.
4. **Find three Reddit threads** asking your question. Answer properly, no link.
5. **Write your DM answer template** — helpful answer, one plain offer, no pressure. Put
   it in `Templates/`.
6. **Do not automate anything** until post 60.
