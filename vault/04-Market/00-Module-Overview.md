## The hard module

Building is work you control. You sit down, you write, it gets done.

Distribution is not like that. You do the work and mostly nothing happens, for weeks, and
the feedback is delayed and noisy. This is the module where people quit — not because it
is intellectually difficult, but because it requires sustained effort with no reward
signal for a long stretch.

Knowing that in advance is most of the battle. The first ten posts get nothing. That is
not failure, it is the beginning, and it is what everyone's beginning looks like.

---

## What this module is for

At the end you have:

- A platform chosen for a reason
- A content system you can sustain, not a burst
- A 30-day calendar with posts already planned
- An email list collecting from day one
- The ability to read your numbers and know what to change

## Start this before you finish building

The one piece of sequencing advice that matters: **do not wait until the course is
finished to start posting.**

Distribution has a long lag. If you start on launch day, your launch is to nobody. If you
start in week two of writing, you have six weeks of compounding before there is anything
to sell — and you will have learned what your audience responds to, which improves the
course.

Posting while building also solves the content problem. Everything you are writing is
material.

---

## The honest expectation

- Posts 1–10: nothing. Genuinely nothing.
- Posts 10–30: a few views, occasional comment.
- Posts 30–60: one does noticeably better. You learn something from it.
- Posts 60+: it starts to compound, if you kept going.

Most people stop at post 12.
