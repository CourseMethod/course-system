# Module 1 — Mindset

**8 lessons · about 90 minutes of reading · one decision at the end**

---

## What this module is for

You finish this module with a topic you have chosen and stopped reconsidering.

That is the entire goal. Not motivation, not confidence, not a business plan — a
decision, written down, that you are prepared to act on for the next two months.

It sounds small. It is the thing most people never actually do. They hold the decision
half-open while trying to make progress on everything downstream of it, and then wonder
why the work feels like wading.

---

## Why this module exists at all

Most people reading this do not need to be convinced that selling a course is possible.
They need three specific obstacles removed:

1. **They cannot see which part of what they know is valuable.** Everything you know
   feels obvious to you. That is what expertise feels like from the inside, and it is
   why people with genuinely teachable skills conclude they have nothing to teach.

2. **They believe "expert" means something it does not.** You are not competing with the
   best in the world. You are competing with the reader's confusion, and you beat it by
   being two years ahead and able to remember what it was like not to know.

3. **They have absorbed a set of claims about this business that are not true**, and
   will make decisions based on them — about timelines, about income, about what is
   allowed. Getting those straight now prevents a lot of wasted months.

## When you are done

You should be able to write this sentence and mean it:

> I am teaching **[who]** how to **[do what]**, so they can **[get what result]**,
> and I know this because **[your actual experience]**.

If you cannot fill in all four blanks, do not move to Module 2. Go back to
[[02-Picking-Your-Topic]] and do the exercise properly.
