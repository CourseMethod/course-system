Email performance

Open rates are unreliable — see the note below.

Apple Mail Privacy Protection and most corporate gateways pre-fetch images, which registers an open nobody performed. Depending on your audience, 20–60% of recorded opens may be machines. Treat open rate as a relative trend between campaigns, never as an absolute. Click rate is far more trustworthy.
