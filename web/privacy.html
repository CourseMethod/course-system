Who is responsible for your data

Course Method is a trading name used by an individual seller based in Croatia. That seller is the data controller for everything described here, and can be reached at coursemethodsales@gmail.com.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

DataWhyLegal basis
Email address To deliver your download link, send your receipt, and handle support Performance of a contract
Purchase record — product, amount, date, Stripe reference To prove what you bought, reissue access, handle refunds, meet accounting obligations Contract and legal obligation
Download activity — when a link was used, how many times To enforce download limits and detect link sharing Legitimate interest in preventing abuse
Page events and IP address Basic first-party analytics — which pages are viewed and which lead to a sale Legitimate interest, or consent where required
Email opens and link clicks To see whether delivery emails are arriving and being read Consent where required

We never see your card details. Payment is processed by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe directly and never touches our server. We receive only the outcome, the amount and a reference.

Analytics and email tracking

This site records basic page events together with an IP address, so we can see which pages lead to a sale. Our emails contain a small invisible image that tells us the message was opened, and links that pass through our server before forwarding you on, so we can tell whether delivery emails are actually arriving. None of this is shared with advertisers.

You can stop the email tracking by viewing our messages with images turned off. If you would rather we did not record your page activity at all, email us and we will exclude you.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers, each processing data on our instructions:

  • Stripe — payment processing and fraud prevention.
  • Railway — hosting for the website and database, servers in the EU.
  • Our email provider — sending delivery and support emails.

Some of these may process data outside the EEA. Where that happens it is covered by the transfer safeguards those providers have in place, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as accounting and tax rules require, which in Croatia is generally several years. Analytics events are kept no longer than needed to be useful. If you ask us to delete your data, we will delete what we are not legally obliged to keep, and tell you what remains and why.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, subject to records we must keep;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including anything based on legitimate interest;
  • give you your data in a portable form;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on consent.

Email coursemethodsales@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. You can opt out of email tracking by viewing our emails with images turned off.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in Croatia that is the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), and if you live elsewhere in the EEA, the authority in your own country.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
