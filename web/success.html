What to do in the next 10 minutes

Genuinely worth doing now rather than later. The people who open this in the first hour are the ones who finish.

  1. Unzip the download

    You'll get a folder of Markdown files. That's the whole course — no installer, no account.

  2. Install Obsidian (free)

    obsidian.md — it turns the folder into a navigable course with working links between lessons. Optional but strongly recommended. Any text editor works otherwise.

  3. Open folder as vault

    In Obsidian, choose Open folder as vault and point it at the folder you unzipped.

  4. Start at the Command Center

    Open 00-Command-Center.md. It's the map — it tells you what to read and in what order, so you never have to guess.

  5. Do one lesson. Not twenty.

    Read 01-Mindset/02-Picking-Your-Topic.md and do the exercise at the bottom. Twelve minutes. It's the decision everything else hangs on, and the single most common place people stall is leaving it open while trying to move ahead.

One thing worth knowing now. Don't read the whole thing first. The people who finish read one lesson, do the thing at the bottom of it, then read the next. The ones who binge all forty over a weekend feel productive and have built nothing by Monday.
